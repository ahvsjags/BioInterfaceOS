For every panel, axes, independent-unit denominator, `n`, and interval method are declared `NOT_APPLICABLE`: no measured quantity is plotted. The legacy numerical figure specifications are kept immutable and listed in the R2 withdrawal ledger; future real-data figures must be supplied by T122–T124.

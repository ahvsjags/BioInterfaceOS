# R4 T218 external evidence handoff status — 2026-08-14

## Decision

T218 closes the public evidence-contract inconsistency discovered after T217. It does not manufacture the missing third-party evidence. The fixed paper-data routes are now explicitly separated into author-run exploratory analysis, paper-attached secondary OOD, no-author reproduction and genuinely independent lockbox evaluation.

## What the public paper data can establish

- The T195/T197/T198 routes can be rerun from the declared paper/accession-derived source maps.
- The T203/T209 routes can be reported as paper-attached secondary OOD or sensitivity evidence under their source-unit and license limits.
- The T217 amendment fixes the primary estimand, availability denominators, missingness policy and project multiplicity ledger before the external handoff.
- The T192 three-laboratory common-target result is a redistributable source-compatibility result; it is not promoted to three independent biological cohorts because the public records do not prove donor-level independence for every anchor.

## What remains unproven

- A non-author protected lockbox evaluator receipt.
- A no-author accession-to-result scientific reproduction.
- Two distinct non-author real-task adoption receipts.
- A DOI/archive locator bound to the exact release.

The protocol and templates are:

- `docs/data/R4_T218_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE_HANDOFF_PROTOCOL.json`;
- `docs/data/R4_T218_EXTERNAL_USER_ADOPTION_INTAKE.json`;

The no-author reproduction path now has one concrete public route: the CC-BY-3.0 PMC6592156 silver-nanoparticle human-plasma supplementary dataset. Its prescribed audit/evaluation commands and comparison-only expected counts are bound in the T218 protocol. This improves the feasibility of obtaining a real external receipt; it does not count as a receipt until a non-author team independently reacquires the files and submits an auditable signed result.

Until these receipts are independently verified, `scientific_submission_ready=false` and a strong-Q1 submission claim is not supported. This is a successful evidence-boundary closure, not a claim that the external gates have already passed.

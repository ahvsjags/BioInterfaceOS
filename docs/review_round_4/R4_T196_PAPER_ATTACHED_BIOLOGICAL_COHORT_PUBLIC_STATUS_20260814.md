Status: `PUBLIC_CC_BY_BIOLOGICAL_COHORT_AUTHOR_OOD`

The public candidate now includes the CC-BY-4.0 paper-attached PXD017052
Supplementary Data 5 workbook and its row-level source-cell map. T180/T181
verification records 141 biological subject units, 705 subject-by-particle
measurement batches, 666 rank-qualified batches and 17,026 external target
observations over 34 shared canonical proteins.

clean checkout can obtain the exact source bytes and rerun the source audit and
the subject-clustered OOD receipt. It does not create another laboratory
anchor. The cohort is from the Seer/Broad laboratory lineage, and the result
remains an author-run exploratory OOD analysis rather than independent
validation, a protected lockbox evaluation, no-author reproduction, or an
external adoption receipt.
